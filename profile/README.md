## 🎯 About the lab

> The **ELTE Cybersecurity Lab**, part of the **Department of Computer Algebra**, studies how complex digital systems fail under attack , and how they can be made more secure in practice.

Our work spans modern machine learning, cloud and identity infrastructures, blockchain ecosystems, applied cryptography, and next-generation mobile networks. Across these areas, our research combines **theoretical foundations with practical experimentation** — attack analysis, protocol design, system implementation, and security evaluation. We believe in open-source development and sharing with the broader community whenever possible.

## 🧩 How we work

Many of our activities are organized as **small-group projects** rather than individual assignments. We believe this format better reflects how strong research and development work happens in cybersecurity: through collaboration across different skills, perspectives, and methods.

A typical team project involves **3 to 5 members** working together over a few months, with complementary roles:

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="25%" align="center" valign="top">
      <h3>🧮</h3>
      <b>Theorist</b>
      <p><sub>Focuses on the theoretical background and threat model.</sub></p>
    </td>
    <td width="25%" align="center" valign="top">
      <h3>⚙️</h3>
      <b>Engineer</b>
      <p><sub>Works on implementation and system integration.</sub></p>
    </td>
    <td width="25%" align="center" valign="top">
      <h3>🐞</h3>
      <b>Attacker</b>
      <p><sub>Works on attacks or adversarial testing.</sub></p>
    </td>
    <td width="25%" align="center" valign="top">
      <h3>📊</h3>
      <b>Evaluator</b>
      <p><sub>Works on evaluation, measurement, and documentation.</sub></p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

Each team is guided by a **professional supervisor** who helps shape the research question, supports planning, and provides technical and methodological direction. We also believe in **open-source development** — contributing reusable code, tools, and experimental insights that others can build on.
